Privacy Policy - Candy Merge

Effective date: November 28, 2026

Developer: Abdelrahman Abed ("we", "us")
Contact: a998.abed@gmail.com

This policy covers the Android game "Candy Merge", a free offline merge puzzle game for ages 13 and up.

1. Summary

  • The game has no accounts, sign-in or chat. We do not collect personal information.
  • Your progress, settings, coins, gems and best scores are stored only on your device.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google's SDK collects some data for ads (see section 3).
  • There are no in-app purchases with real money.

2. Data we collect

None. Progress, settings, coins, gems and best scores are kept only on your device. The game does not send them to us, and we do not collect personal information. Uninstalling the game deletes all local game data.

3. Data collected by Google AdMob

The game uses Google AdMob to show interstitial ads and opt-in rewarded ads. It does not show banner ads. Google may collect and process, as described in Google's own policies:

  • Advertising ID
  • IP address
  • Device and app information
  • Ad interaction data

Google uses this data for ad delivery, measurement, frequency capping and fraud prevention. Personalized ads apply only where consent or the law allows. See Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

4. Consent and your choices

If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before any ads load. You can change your choice later from Settings.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

6. Data retention and deletion

We hold no user data. Uninstalling the game deletes all local game data. To manage data held by Google, use your Google account's privacy controls and Android's advertising ID settings.

7. Security

Game data stays on your device and is not transmitted to us. We have no server that holds your data.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change, and the latest version is always at https://abdelrahman0abed.github.io/candy-merge/privacy.html.
