Privacy Policy - Overdrive Survivor: Endless Horde

Effective date: 2026-11-24

Developer: Abdelrahman Abed ("we", "us")
Contact: a998.abed@gmail.com

This policy covers the Android game "Overdrive Survivor: Endless Horde" (package com.abdelrahmanabed.overdrivesurvivor).

1. Summary

  • The game has no accounts and no server. We do not collect, store or receive any personal data.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your device, in an encrypted local save file.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google's SDK collects some data for ads, analytics and fraud prevention (see section 3).
  • There are no in-app purchases.

2. Data we collect

None. The developer does not operate any server or database, and does not receive your progress, name, email, location or any identifier. Game progress (currencies, gear, talents, settings) is saved locally and is removed when you uninstall the game or use Settings > Reset progress.

3. Data collected by Google AdMob

The game uses the Google AdMob SDK to show rewarded and interstitial ads. Google may collect and process, as described in Google's own policies:

  • Advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it
  • App interactions (for example ad impressions and clicks)
  • Diagnostics and crash information about the SDK and the app

Google uses this data to serve and measure ads, for analytics, and for fraud prevention and security. Google acts as an independent controller of this data. See Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses data from apps that use its services. Data is encrypted in transit.

4. Consent and your choices

On first launch (where required, for example in the EEA, UK and some US states) the game shows a Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent prompt. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings > Privacy options.

You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads. Declining personalization does not remove ads; they will be non-personalized.

5. Children

The game is not designed for or directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. It is not part of Google Play's Families program. The game requests ads treated as not directed to under-age users (13+ audience).

6. Data retention and deletion

We hold no user data. To delete local game data, use Settings > Reset progress or uninstall the game. To manage data held by Google, use your Google account's privacy controls.

7. Security

The local save file is encrypted. Network traffic from the ad SDK uses encryption in transit.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change, and the latest version is always at https://abdelrahman0abed.github.io/overdrive-survivor/privacy.html.
